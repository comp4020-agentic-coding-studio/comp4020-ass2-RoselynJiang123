## The claim

A trial with a sham arm must agree before it starts when it will stop, who
watches the interim data, when a patient is unblinded, and what the sham arm
is offered at the end. These decisions are hardest to make when the answer
is already visible, which is why they are written down first.

## Before the Round

Read the role of the data monitoring committee in your precedent trial. Find
out what, if anything, its sham arm was offered afterwards.

## In the room

Tabletop. A data monitoring committee meets three times with fictional
interim results. At each meeting it decides whether to continue, and the
room decides whether that decision was written down before or after the
data.

## Protocol log

what the sham arm is offered.
