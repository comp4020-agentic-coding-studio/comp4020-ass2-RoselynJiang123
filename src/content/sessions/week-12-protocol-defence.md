## The claim

Everything in the protocol has been written by someone who wants the trial
to happen. This Round gives it to people who do not have to.

## Before the Round

Submit A2. Read the protocol you have been allocated to review for A3.

## In the room

Each protocol goes before a panel of three classmates sitting as the Ethics
Committee, with the convenor in the chair. Ten minutes to present, fifteen to
answer. The panel does not say whether it approves. That is A3.

## Protocol log

**§12 Defence notes.** The three questions you expected, the three you were
asked, and the difference.
