## The claim

To a methodologist the Ladder climbs towards better evidence. To an ethics
committee it climbs towards more risk, offered to someone who, by design,
cannot benefit. Both readings are correct. A sham trial is justified only
when there is real uncertainty about the procedure, the knowledge is worth
having, and the risk of the chosen rung is as small as the question allows.

## Before the Round

Read Macklin (1999) against placebo surgery, Freeman et al. (1999) in its
defence, and Horng and Miller (2002) on the conditions under which it can be
acceptable.

## In the room

The committee sits. The class reviews three anonymised proposals, at L2, L4
and L5, and must approve, approve with conditions, or decline each one.
Minutes are taken.

## Protocol log

**§7 Risk justification.** Your rung, the risks it adds over the rung below
it, and why the answer is worth them.
