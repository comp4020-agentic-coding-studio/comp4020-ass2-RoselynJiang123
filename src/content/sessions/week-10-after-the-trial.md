## The claim

After the 2009 vertebroplasty trials, guidance turned against the procedure.
Then VAPOUR (2016) reported benefit in acute fractures, and VERTOS IV (2018)
did not. Knee arthroscopy for degenerative disease followed a similar path,
from Moseley (2002) through FIDELITY (2013) to a strong recommendation
against it in 2017. Practice moved more slowly than the evidence, and for
reasons a protocol can anticipate: payment, training, patient demand, and the
argument that the trial studied the wrong patients.

## Before the Round

Read VAPOUR and VERTOS IV side by side. Read the 2017 BMJ Rapid
Recommendation on knee arthroscopy.

## In the room

Write the paragraph a guideline panel would write if your trial came back
null. Swap it with a neighbour, and write the reply an unconvinced surgeon
would send.
