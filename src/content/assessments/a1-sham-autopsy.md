## The brief

> Take apart one published sham-controlled trial and place it on the Ladder.

Choose one published sham-controlled trial of a procedure. Any trial in the
course registry except the five landmark trials is approved; others need the
convenor's agreement before Week 6.

## What you submit

Submit 1,200 words and a one-page rating sheet. The rating sheet places the
trial on the Sham Fidelity Ladder and lists, for each element of the rung,
the sentence in the paper that shows it was there. The essay defends the
rating, analyses the four blinding channels, and states what the trial could
and could not conclude because of how its sham was built.
