You are allocated one classmate's protocol at the Week 12 defence. You have now heard it defended. Write the committee's response, as one of its members.

The whole course has been about building something that should survive scrutiny. This assessment asks you to supply the scrutiny, fairly.

## What you write

Choose one decision and argue for it:

- **Approve.**
- **Approve with conditions.** List each condition as a change the author must make, in a form they could act on.
- **Decline.** State what would have to change for a resubmission to succeed.

Your response must:

- refer to the protocol's rung and its risk justification (§4 and §7);
- raise at least one issue about blinding or consent;
- distinguish between what is required and what is suggested;
- be addressed to the author, not about them.

## Suggested structure

| Paragraph | Content | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The decision, and the single reason that decided it | 60 |
| 2 | The rung and the risk: is the exchange justified? | 120 |
| 3 | Blinding and consent: the most serious issue | 120 |
| 4 | Conditions or requirements, numbered | 100 |

## Marking criteria

This assessment is marked on one question: **if the author read your review, would the protocol get better?**

| Band | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| High Distinction (80+) | The review identifies the protocol's most important weakness, explains it precisely, and gives conditions the author could act on tomorrow. The decision follows from the argument. |
| Distinction–Credit (60–79) | Real issues identified and explained; conditions mostly actionable; decision reasonable. |
| Pass (50–59) | Issues identified but described in general terms; conditions vague. |
| Not yet (<50) | Summarises the protocol, praises or dismisses it, or does not reach a decision. |

## Rules

- Review the protocol you were allocated, not your own and not a friend's by arrangement.
- Do not share your review with the author before marking.
- The standard is the protocol as submitted, not as defended. If the defence resolved an issue, say so, and require the change in writing.

## Questions

**Can I decline a protocol I think is good?** Only if you can say what would have to change. A decision without reasons is not a review.

**Does my decision affect the author's A2 mark?** No. A2 is marked independently before reviews are released.

Late work and extensions follow the course [policies](/policies/). Because reviews are released to authors together, extensions for A3 are limited to three working days.
