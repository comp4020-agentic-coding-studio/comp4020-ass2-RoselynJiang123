## Summary

Five trials, five specialties, one structure. This lecture reads the landmark sham-controlled trials side by side and shows that they are, in the ways that matter, the same trial five times: a procedure in wide use, a plausible mechanism, a sham arm built to carry the ritual without the mechanism, and a result that its field then had to live with.

## Outline

1. **The shared structure.** Why the specialty is detail and the design is the lesson.
2. **What each sham actually did.** Skin incisions and simulated sounds in Moseley 2002; a needle resting on bone and cement mixed in the room in the vertebroplasty trials; sedation and headphones in ORBITA; angiography alone in SYMPLICITY HTN-3; burr holes that did not pass through the skull in Freed 2001.
3. **Who was blinded in each.** The patient almost always; the operator almost never; the assessor and analyst depending on how carefully the trial was run.
4. **What each result changed, and what it didn't.** A preview of Week 10.
